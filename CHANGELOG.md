## [0.2.0](https://github.com/vsh1ft/ground-segment-api/compare/f979c42...v0.2.0) (2026-11-03)

First HTTP API: ground stations and satellite pass predictions.

### Added

* `GET /passes?satellite=&station=&duration=`: predicted passes (rise, peak, set) of a satellite over a station,
  in UTC, starting now; up to 7 days ahead.
* Stations: `GET /stations`, `GET /stations/{id}`, `POST /stations`, `PUT /stations/{id}`,
  `DELETE /stations/{id}`, each with its own minimum elevation.
* RFC 9457 problem details for errors (400, 404, 409, 503); validation errors list every invalid field.
* OpenAPI description at `/v3/api-docs` and Swagger UI at `/swagger-ui.html`.

### Changed

* The app runs as a long-lived service instead of printing passes once and exiting.
* An unknown satellite gives 404; CelesTrak being unreachable with no stored data gives 503.
